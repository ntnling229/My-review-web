1. Why Logic? & Formal Logic (邏輯動機與形式邏輯)

What one says and does have to make sense. Logic is the basis of reasoning.

• 矛盾例子（矛與盾）：「吾盾之堅，物莫能陷也」與「吾矛之利，於物無不陷也」同時成立會產生邏輯矛盾（不可同世而立）。

• Smullyan 騎士與無賴謎題：島上有永遠說真話的 knight 與永遠說謊的 knave。遇到 A 與 B，若 A 說「B 是 knight」，且 B 說「我們兩人是相反類型（opposite types）」，即可透過真值推理判定 A 與 B 的身分。

• 《孫子算經》雞兔同籠（1500 年前）：上有 35 頭、下有 94 足。算式：$(94 - 35 \times 2) \div 2 = 12$（兔子數），總頭數 $35 - 12 = 23$（雞數），亦可設未知數 $x$ 與 $y$ 列式求解。

2. Propositions (命題，Page 2)

Definition of Proposition (命題的定義)

A proposition (命題) is a declarative statement that is true (T) or false (F), but not both.（即：表示判斷的陳述語句稱為命題；真值為 T，假值為 F。）

1. Washington, D.C. is the capital of the United States of America.（T）

2. Toronto is the capital of Canada.（F）

3. $1 + 1 = 2$（T）

4. $2 + 2 = 3$（F）

1. What time is it now?（非陳述句）

2. Read it carefully!（非陳述句／祈使句）

3. $x + 1 = 2$（既非真亦非假，視 $x$ 取值而定）

4. $x + y = z$（既非真亦非假，視變數取值而定）

5. Applications of Logic: Translating English Sentences (邏輯應用與語句翻譯，Page 17)

條件與充要語句對照表

• if $p$ then $q$ / $q$ if $p$ / $p$ is sufficient for $q$: $p \rightarrow q$（$q$ is necessary for $p$）。

• $p$ only if $q$: $p \rightarrow q$；例："$x = 0$ and $y = 0$ only if $x + y = 0$"。

• $p$ if and only if $q$ (sufficient and necessary): $p \leftrightarrow q$；例："$x = 0$ and $y = 0$ if and only if $x + y = 0$"。

翻譯："You can access the internet from the campus only if you are a computer science major or you are not a freshman."

設 $a$: "You can access the internet from the campus."，$b$: "You are a computer science major."，$c$: "You are a freshman."

• only if 版本：$a \text{ only if } (b \vee \neg c) = a \rightarrow (b \vee \neg c)$。

• if 版本："You can access the internet from the campus if you are a computer science major or you are not a freshman." $\implies a \text{ if } (b \vee \neg c) = (b \vee \neg c) \rightarrow a$。

翻譯："You cannot ride the roller coaster if you are under 4 feet tall unless you are older than 16 years old."

設 $q$: "You can ride the roller coaster."，$r$: "You are under 4 feet tall."，$s$: "You are older than 16 years old."

可表示為 $(r \wedge \neg s) \rightarrow \neg q$ 或 $\neg s \rightarrow (r \rightarrow \neg q)$。

$p$ unless $q$ ($p$ except if $q$) 規則：$\neg q \rightarrow p$（與 $p \vee q$ 相同）。

• 例 A：$p$: "we have on line class"，$q$: "COVID under control" $\implies$ "we have on line class unless COVID under control" $\equiv$ "if COVID not under control we have on line class"。

• 例 B：$p$: "$x + y = 0$"，$q$: "$x \neq 0$ or $y \neq 0$" $\implies$ "$x + y = 0$ unless $x \neq 0$ or $y \neq 0$" $\equiv$ "if $x = 0$ and $y = 0$ then $x + y = 0$"。

1. Tautology, Contradiction & Contingency (永真、永假與中性公式，Page 21)

Definition 1 (see page 21)

• (1) Tautology (永真公式): A compound proposition that is always true (T), no matter what the truth values of the propositions that occur in it（例如 $p \vee \neg p$）。

• (2) Contradiction (永假公式): A compound proposition that is always false (F), no matter what the truth values of the propositions that occur in it（例如 $p \wedge \neg p$）。

• (3) Contingency (中性公式): A proposition that is neither a tautology nor a contradiction（真值隨組成命題而有真有假）。

Example 1 (page 21): 永真公式恆為真（T），永假公式恆為假（F）。

1. Introduction (引言，Page 36)

Question: The meaning of some statements cannot be expressed in propositional logic. How to extend?（部分語句的語意無法用命題邏輯表達，需引入謂詞與量詞加以擴充。）

2. Predicates (謂詞，Page 38)

謂詞與命題函數（Propositional Function）概念

• 在語句 $"x \text{ is greater than } 3"$ 中，$x$ 是主詞（subject），"is greater than 3" 是謂詞（predicate）。

• 記作 $P(x)$，其中 $P$ 為謂詞（"is greater than 3"），$P(x)$ 稱為命題函數 $P$ 在 $x$ 處的值（value of propositional function $P$ at $x$）。

• 一般而言，$P(x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n)$ 是命題函數 $P$ 在 $n$ 元組 $(x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n)$ 處的值，$P$ 稱為 $n$ 元謂詞。

• $P(x)$ 為 "$x$ is greater than $3$"：$P(4)$ 為 true，$P(2)$ 為 false。

• Example 3 (page 38): $Q(x, y)$ 為 "$x = y + 3$"；$Q(1, 2)$ 為 false，$Q(3, 0)$ 為 true。

3. Universal Quantifier (全稱量詞，Page 40 - 42)

個體域與全稱量詞定義（Definition 1, Page 40）

• Universe of Discourse（個體域／論域，亦稱 domain）：包含變數所有可能取值的集合。

• 全稱量詞 $\forall x\, P(x)$：表示「個體域中所有 $x$ 皆使 $P(x)$ 為真」，讀作 "for all $x\, P(x)$" 或 "for every $x\, P(x)$"。

• 有限個體域等價式：若個體域為有限集合 $\{x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n\}$，則 $\forall x\, P(x)$ 等價於 $P(x_1) \wedge P(x_2) \wedge \dots \wedge P(x_n)$。

• 找反例（Counterexample）：要證明 $\forall x\, P(x)$ 為假，只需找出個體域中一個使 $P(x)$ 為假的反例即可。

• Example 8 (page 41): $P(x)$ 為 "$x + 1 > x$"，個體域為全體實數 $\implies \forall x\, P(x)$ 為 true。

• Example 9 (page 41): $Q(x)$ 為 "$x < 2$"，個體域為全體實數 $\implies \forall x\, Q(x)$ 為 false（例如 $x = 2$ 或 $x = 3$ 時不成立）。

• Example 11 (page 42): $P(x)$ 為 "$x^2 < 10$"，個體域為不高於 $4$ 的正整數 $\{1, 2, 3, 4\}$。$\forall x\, P(x) \equiv P(1) \wedge P(2) \wedge P(3) \wedge P(4)$，因 $4^2 = 16 \not< 10$，故為 false。

• Example 13 (page 42): 考慮 $\forall x(x^2 \ge x)$。$x^2 \ge x \iff x(x - 1) \ge 0 \iff x \ge 1 \text{ 或 } x \le 0$。在整數域上為 true；在實數域上為 false（例如 $0 < x < 1$ 時 $x^2 < x$）。

• Counterexample 範例：$P(x)$ 為 "$x^2 > 0$"，個體域為全體整數。取 $x = 0$ 時 $0^2 > 0$ 為假，因此 $\forall x\, P(x)$ 為 false。

4. Existential Quantifier & Summary (存在量詞與總結，Page 42 - 43)

存在量詞定義（Definition 2, Page 42）與有限個體域等價式

• 存在量詞 $\exists x\, P(x)$：表示「個體域中存在至少一個 $x$ 使 $P(x)$ 為真」，讀作 "There is an $x$ such that $P(x)$"、"There is at least one $x$ such that $P(x)$" 或 "For some $x\, P(x)$"。

• 有限個體域等價式：若個體域為有限集合 $\{x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n\}$，則 $\exists x\, P(x)$ 等價於 $P(x_1) \vee P(x_2) \vee \dots \vee P(x_n)$。

• Example 14 (page 43): $P(x)$ 為 "$x > 3$"，個體域為全體實數 $\implies \exists x\, P(x)$ 為 true（例如 $x = 3.5$ 或 $x = 4$）。

• Example 15 (page 43): $Q(x)$ 為 "$x = x + 1$"，個體域為全體實數。對所有實數皆為假，故 $\exists x\, Q(x)$ 為 false。

• Example 16 (page 43): $P(x)$ 為 "$x^2 > 10$"，個體域為 $\{1, 2, 3, 4\}$。$\exists x\, P(x) \equiv P(1) \vee P(2) \vee P(3) \vee P(4)$，因 $P(4)$（$16 > 10$）為真，故為 true。

Statement (語句) When True? (何時為真) When False? (何時為假)
$\forall x\, P(x)$ $P(x)$ is true for all $x$. There is an $x$ for which $P(x)$ is false.
$\exists x\, P(x)$ There is an $x$ for which $P(x)$ is true. $P(x)$ is false for every $x$.

5. Restricted Domain & Binding Variables (受限個體域與變數約束，Page 44)

受限個體域改寫規則與約束／自由變數

• 在實數域下，限制條件加入全稱量詞常以蘊含（$\rightarrow$）表示；加入存在量詞常以合取（$\wedge$）表示。

• Bound Variable（約束變數）：受量詞（$\forall$ 或 $\exists$）作用並指明作用範圍（scope）的變數；Free Variable（自由變數）：未受量詞約束的變數。

• Example 17 (page 44, 個體域為實數)：

$\forall x < 0\ (x^2 > 0) \equiv \forall x\ (x < 0 \rightarrow x^2 > 0)$

$\forall y \neq 0\ (y^3 \neq 0) \equiv \forall y\ (y \neq 0 \rightarrow y^3 \neq 0)$

$\exists z > 0\ (z^2 = 2) \equiv \exists z\ (z > 0 \wedge z^2 = 2)$

• Example 18 (Binding Variables)：

(a) $\exists x(x + y = 1)$：$x$ 為 bound variable，$y$ 為 free variable。

(b) $\exists x(P(x) \vee Q(x)) \vee \forall x\, R(x)$：兩個量詞各自約束其作用域（scope）內的 $x$。

6. Logical Equivalence of Predicates (謂詞邏輯等價，Page 45)

謂詞與量詞邏輯等價定義（Definition 3, Page 45）

• 含有謂詞與量詞的語句 $S$ 與 $T$ 稱為邏輯等價（logically equivalent），記作 $S \equiv T$，當且僅當無論將何種謂詞代入及選取何種個體域，$S$ 與 $T$ 恆具有相同的真值。

• 命題等價式可提升至謂詞層級，例如 $P(x) \wedge Q(x) \equiv Q(x) \wedge P(x)$。

需證明雙向蘊含：(1) 若 $\forall x(P(x) \wedge Q(x))$ 為真，則 $\forall x\, P(x) \wedge \forall x\, Q(x)$ 為真；(2) 若 $\forall x\, P(x) \wedge \forall x\, Q(x)$ 為真，則 $\forall x(P(x) \wedge Q(x))$ 為真。

• (1) 正向推導：假設 $\forall x(P(x) \wedge Q(x))$ 為真。對個體域中任一元素 $a$，$P(a) \wedge Q(a)$ 為真，故 $P(a)$ 與 $Q(a)$ 皆為真。因對所有元素皆成立，故 $\forall x\, P(x)$ 與 $\forall x\, Q(x)$ 皆為真，即 $\forall x\, P(x) \wedge \forall x\, Q(x)$ 為真。

• (2) 逆向推導：假設 $\forall x\, P(x) \wedge \forall x\, Q(x)$ 為真，則 $\forall x\, P(x)$ 與 $\forall x\, Q(x)$ 皆為真。對個體域中任一元素 $a$，$P(a)$ 與 $Q(a)$ 皆為真，故 $P(a) \wedge Q(a)$ 對所有 $a$ 為真，即 $\forall x(P(x) \wedge Q(x))$ 為真。

• 結論：$\forall x(P(x) \wedge Q(x)) \equiv \forall x\, P(x) \wedge \forall x\, Q(x)$。

7. Negating Quantified Expressions (量詞式否定，Page 41, 48)

量詞否定等價律（De Morgan's Laws for Quantifiers）

• $\neg \forall x\, P(x) \equiv \exists x\, \neg P(x)$（「全體皆非」與「存在不成立」等價）。

• $\neg \exists x\, Q(x) \equiv \forall x\, \neg Q(x)$（「不存在」與「全體皆不成立」等價）。

• 修習微積分例子："Every student in the class has taken a course in calculus" 為 $\forall x\, P(x)$，其否定為 $\exists x\, \neg P(x)$（"There is a student in the class who has not taken a course in calculus"）；"There is a student in the class who has taken a course in calculus" 為 $\exists x\, Q(x)$，其否定為 $\forall x\, \neg Q(x)$。

• Example 21 (page 41):

(1) $\neg \forall x(x^2 > x) \equiv \exists x\, \neg(x^2 > x) \equiv \exists x(x^2 \le x)$（可代入 $[0.5, 3]$ 與 $[2, 5]$ 檢驗真值）。

(2) $\neg \exists x(x^2 = 2) \equiv \forall x\, \neg(x^2 = 2) \equiv \forall x(x^2 \neq 2)$（可代入 $[0, 1]$ 與 $[1, 2]$ 檢驗真值）。

• Example 22 (page 48) 證明：證明 $\neg \forall x(P(x) \rightarrow Q(x))$ 與 $\exists x(P(x) \wedge \neg Q(x))$ 邏輯等價：

$\neg \forall x(P(x) \rightarrow Q(x)) \equiv \exists x\, \neg(P(x) \rightarrow Q(x)) \equiv \exists x(P(x) \wedge \neg Q(x))$。

8. Translating English to Logical Expressions & Exercises (語句翻譯與練習，Page 48 - 49)

翻譯建模要點

• 當個體域設為全體人（all people）時，「所有 $S(x)$ 皆滿足 $C(x)$」通常用 $\forall x(S(x) \rightarrow C(x))$（避免誤用 $\forall x(S(x) \wedge C(x))$，後者會要求所有人都是該班學生且都修過微積分）。

• 當個體域設為全體人時，「存在 $S(x)$ 滿足 $M(x)$」通常用 $\exists x(S(x) \wedge M(x))$（避免誤用 $\exists x(S(x) \rightarrow M(x))$）。

• Example 23 (page 48): Express the statement "Every student in this class has studied calculus" in predicates and quantifiers.

Way 1: 設 $C(x)$ 為 "$x$ has studied calculus"，domain 為 all the students in the class $\implies \forall x\, C(x)$。

Way 2: 設 domain 為 all people，$S(x)$ 為 "person $x$ is in this class"，$C(x)$ 為 "person $x$ has studied calculus"（"For every person $x$, if person $x$ is in this class then $x$ has studied calculus."）$\implies \forall x(S(x) \rightarrow C(x))$（correct；$\forall x(S(x) \wedge C(x))$ wrong）。

• Example 24 (page 49): Express the statements below in predicates and quantifiers:

(1) "Some students in this class has visited Mexico"

(2) "Every student in this class has visited Canada or Mexico"

Answer (a) — For (1):

Way 1: 設 $M(x)$ 為 "$x$ has visited Mexico"，domain 為 all the students in the class $\implies \exists x\, M(x)$。

Way 2: 設 domain 為 all people，$S(x)$ 為 "$x$ is a student in this class"，$M(x)$ 為 "$x$ has visited Mexico" $\implies \exists x(S(x) \wedge M(x))$（correct；$\exists x(S(x) \rightarrow M(x))$ wrong）。

Answer (b) — For (2):

Way 1: 設 $C(x)$ 為 "$x$ has visited Canada"，$M(x)$ 為 "$x$ has visited Mexico"，domain 為 all the students in the class $\implies \forall x(C(x) \vee M(x))$。

Way 2: 設 domain 為 all people，$S(x)$ 為 "$x$ is a student in this class"，$C(x)$ 為 "$x$ has visited Canada"，$M(x)$ 為 "$x$ has visited Mexico" $\implies \forall x(S(x) \rightarrow (C(x) \vee M(x)))$。

§ 1.5 Nested Quantifiers (量詞嵌套)

Page 57 - 63

1. Introduction (引言，Page 57 - 58)

Nested Quantifiers (量詞嵌套): Quantifiers that occur within the scope of other quantifiers（出現在其他量詞作用域內的量詞），例如 $\forall x \exists y(x + y = 0)$。

• Example 1 (page 57, domain for $x, y, z$ = all real numbers)：

1. $\forall x \forall y (x + y = y + x)$（Commutative law，加法交換律）$\implies$ true

2. $\forall x \exists y (x + y = 0)$（加法反元素存在性）$\implies$ true

3. $\forall x \forall y \forall z (x + (y + z) = (x + y) + z)$（Associative law，加法結合律）$\implies$ true

• Example 2 (page 58, domain = all real numbers)：

$\forall x \forall y ((x > 0) \wedge (y < 0) \rightarrow (xy < 0))$（正數與負數之積恒為負數）$\implies$ true

2. The Order of Quantifiers (量詞的順序，Page 58 - 60)

量詞順序交換規則與蘊含關係

• 同類量詞可交換順序：$\forall x \forall y\, P(x, y) \equiv \forall y \forall x\, P(x, y)$，且 $\exists x \exists y\, P(x, y) \equiv \exists y \exists x\, P(x, y)$。

• 不同類量詞不可隨意交換順序：$\exists y \forall x\, P(x, y) \not\equiv \forall x \exists y\, P(x, y)$。

• 單向蘊含關係（Further, Page 60）：

(a) 若 $\exists y \forall x\, P(x, y)$ 為真，則 $\forall x \exists y\, P(x, y)$ 必為真（即 $\exists y \forall x\, P(x, y) \rightarrow \forall x \exists y\, P(x, y)$ 為永真）。

(b) 若 $\forall x \exists y\, P(x, y)$ 為真，則 $\exists y \forall x\, P(x, y)$ 未必為真（參見第 113 頁 Exercise 28 與 30）。

Statement (語句) When True? (何時為真) When False? (何時為假)
$\forall x \forall y\, P(x, y)$
$\forall y \forall x\, P(x, y)$		 $P(x, y)$ is true for every pair $x, y$. There is a pair $x, y$ for which $P(x, y)$ is false.
$\forall x \exists y\, P(x, y)$ For every $x$ there is a $y$ for which $P(x, y)$ is true. There is an $x$ such that $P(x, y)$ is false for every $y$.
$\exists x \forall y\, P(x, y)$ There is an $x$ for which $P(x, y)$ is true for every $y$. For every $x$ there is a $y$ for which $P(x, y)$ is false.
$\exists x \exists y\, P(x, y)$
$\exists y \exists x\, P(x, y)$		 There is a pair $x, y$ for which $P(x, y)$ is true. $P(x, y)$ is false for every pair $x, y$.

• Example 3 (page 58): 設 $P(x, y)$ 為 "$x + y = y + x$"，所有變數的個體域為全體實數。

$\forall x \forall y\, P(x, y)$ 為 true

$\forall y \forall x\, P(x, y)$ 為 true

故 $\forall x \forall y\, P(x, y) \equiv \forall y \forall x\, P(x, y)$

• Example 4 (page 59): 設 $Q(x, y)$ 為 "$x + y = 0$"，個體域為全體實數，比較 $\exists y \forall x\, Q(x, y)$ 與 $\forall x \exists y\, Q(x, y)$：

(a) $\exists y \forall x\, Q(x, y)$："There is a real number $y$ such that for every real number $x$, $Q(x, y)$."

（存在一個固定的實數 $y$ 使所有實數 $x$ 皆滿足 $x + y = 0$）$\implies$ false。

(b) $\forall x \exists y\, Q(x, y)$："For every real number $x$ there is a real number $y$ such that $Q(x, y)$."

（對每個實數 $x$ 皆可找到對應的 $y = -x$ 使 $x + y = 0$）$\implies$ true。

(c) 因此 $\exists y \forall x\, Q(x, y) \not\equiv \forall x \exists y\, Q(x, y)$（not equivalent）。

• Example 5 (page 59): 設 $Q(x, y, z)$ 為 "$x + y = z$"，個體域為全體實數：

$\forall x \forall y \exists z\, Q(x, y, z)$ 為 true（取 $z = x + y$）；

而 $\exists z \forall x \forall y\, Q(x, y, z)$ 為 false（不存在固定常數 $z$ 等於所有 $x + y$）。

3. Translating Mathematical Statements (數學命題翻譯，Page 60)

4. Translating Nested Quantifiers into English (邏輯公式轉自然語言，Page 61)

解讀嵌套量詞公式時，由外而內逐層將 $\forall$ 與 $\exists$ 還原為對應個體域的自然語言描述。

• Example 9 (page 61): 其中 $C(x)$ 為 "$x$ has a computer"，$F(x, y)$ 為 "$x, y$ are friends"，個體域為全校所有學生（all students in the school）。

$\forall x (C(x) \vee \exists y (C(y) \wedge F(x, y)))$

語意：學校裡的每一位學生，要嘛自己有一台電腦，要嘛有一位擁有電腦的朋友（Every student in your school has a computer or has a friend who has a computer）。

• Example 10 (page 61): 其中 $F(a, b)$ 為 "$a$ and $b$ are friends"，個體域為全校所有學生。

$\exists x \forall y \forall z ((F(x, y) \wedge F(x, z) \wedge (y \neq z)) \rightarrow \neg F(y, z))$

語意：學校裡存在某位學生 $x$，他／她任意兩位不同的朋友 $y$ 與 $z$ 彼此之間都不是朋友（There is a student none of whose friends are also friends with each other）。

5. Translating English Sentences into Logical Expressions (自然語言轉邏輯式，Page 62)

「唯一性（Uniqueness / Exactly One）」表達技巧

• 要表示「對某個 $x$ 存在唯一一個 $y$ 滿足性質 $B(x, y)$」，寫作：$\exists y (B(x, y) \wedge \forall z ((z \neq y) \rightarrow \neg B(x, z)))$（即存在 $y$ 滿足條件，且任何異於 $y$ 的 $z$ 皆不滿足該條件）。

• Example 11 (page 62): "If a person is female and is parent, then this person is someone's mother."（domain = all people）

改寫："For every person $x$, if person $x$ is a female and person $x$ is a parent, then there exists a person $y$ such that person $x$ is the mother of person $y$."

設：$F(x)$: "$x$ is female"，$P(x)$: "$x$ is a parent"，$M(x, y)$: "$x$ is the mother of $y$"

$\forall x ((F(x) \wedge P(x)) \rightarrow \exists y\, M(x, y))$ 或 $\forall x \exists y ((F(x) \wedge P(x)) \rightarrow M(x, y))$

• Example 12 (page 62): "Everyone has exactly one best friend"（domain = all people）

改寫："For every person $x$, person $x$ has exactly one best friend."

設：$B(x, y)$: "$y$ is the best friend of $x$"

$\forall x \exists y (B(x, y) \wedge \forall z ((z \neq y) \rightarrow \neg B(x, z)))$

6. Negating Nested Quantifiers & Discussion (嵌套量詞的否定，Page 63)

對嵌套量詞取否定時，由最外層量詞開始，逐層向右套用量詞否定等價律（$\neg \forall \equiv \exists \neg$、$\neg \exists \equiv \forall \neg$），直到沒有否定符號出現在任何量詞之前。

將 $\neg \forall x \exists y (xy = 1)$ 化簡使沒有否定符號位於量詞前：

$\neg \forall x \exists y (xy = 1) \equiv \exists x \neg \exists y (xy = 1) \equiv \exists x \forall y \neg(xy = 1) \equiv \exists x \forall y (xy \neq 1)$

§ 2.2 Set Operations (集合運算)

1. Introduction & Basic Operations (基本運算定義)

Definition 1 (page 127): Union (聯集)

Let A and B be sets. The union of the sets A and B, denoted by $A \cup B$, is the set that contains those elements that either in A or in B, or in both.

$A \cup B = \{x \mid x \in A \vee x \in B\}$

Example: $\{1,3,5\} \cup \{1,2,3\} = \{1,2,3,5\}$

Definition 2: Intersection (交集)

Let A and B be sets. The intersection of the sets A and B, denoted by $A \cap B$, is the set containing those elements in both A and B.

$A \cap B = \{x \mid x \in A \wedge x \in B\}$

Example: $\{1,3,5\} \cap \{1,2,3\} = \{1,3\}$

Definition 3 (page 128): Disjoint (互斥集)

Two sets are called disjoint if their intersection is the empty set.

Example 5 (page 128): Let $A = \{1,3,5,7,9\}$ and $B = \{2,4,6,8,10\}$. Since $A \cap B = \emptyset$, A and B are disjoint.

Cardinality of Union (有限集合聯集的基數)

For two finite sets A and B, we have:

$|A \cup B| = |A| + |B| - |A \cap B|$

Definition 4 (page 128): The difference of two sets (差集)

Let A and B be sets. The difference of A and B, denoted by $A - B$, is the set that containing those elements that are in A but not in B. The difference of A and B is also called the complement of B with respect to A (關於A的集合B的補集).

$A - B = \{x \mid x \in A \wedge x \text{ not in } B\}$

Definition 5 (page 129): Complement of a set (補集)

Let U be universal set (全集). The complement of the set A, denoted by $\sim A$, is the complement of A with respect to U.

$\sim A = U - A = \{x \mid x \text{ not in } A\}$

2. Set Identities (集合的恒等式)

Table 1 (page 130): Set Identity Laws (常見集合恒等式)
• Identity law (同一律): $A \cup \emptyset = A$, $A \cap U = A$
• Domination Law (零律): $A \cup U = U$, $A \cap \emptyset = \emptyset$
• Idempotent law (冪等律): $A \cup A = A$, $A \cap A = A$
• Complementation law (雙重否定律): $\sim(\sim A) = A$
• Commutative law (交換律): $A \cup B = B \cup A$, $A \cap B = B \cap A$
• Associative law (結合律): $A \cup (B \cup C) = (A \cup B) \cup C$
• Distributive law (分配律): $A \cup (B \cap C) = (A \cup B) \cap (A \cup C)$
• De Morgan's law (德摩根律): $\sim(A \cup B) = \sim A \cap \sim B$, $\sim(A \cap B) = \sim A \cup \sim B$
• Absorption laws (吸收律): $A \cup (A \cap B) = A$, $A \cap (A \cup B) = A$
• Complement laws: $A \cup \sim A = U$ (排中律), $A \cap \sim A = \emptyset$ (矛盾律)

1. Introduction & Basic Definitions (基本定義與概念)

Definition 1 (page 139): What is a Function? (函數定義)

Let $A$ and $B$ be sets. A function from $A$ to $B$ is an assignment of exactly one element of $B$ to each element of $A$.

We write $f(a) = b$ if $b$ is the unique element of $B$ assigned by the function $f$ to the element $a$ of $A$. If $f$ is a function from $A$ to $B$, we write $f: A \to B$.

Function Assignment Diagram (教材基本概念圖重現)
Definition 2 (page 139): Domain, Codomain, Image, Pre-image & Range (定義域、共域與值域)

If $f$ is a function from $A$ to $B$:

  • $A$ is called the domain (定義域) of $f$.
  • $B$ is called the codomain (共域) of $f$.
  • If $f(a) = b$, $b$ is called the image (像) of $a$, and $a$ is a pre-image (原像) of $b$.
  • The range (值域) of $f$ is the set of all images of elements of $A$.
  • Also, if $f$ is a function from $A$ to $B$, we say $f$ maps $A$ to $B$.

Question:

Let $f: \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z}$ assign the square of an integer to this integer, i.e., $f(x) = x^2$. What is the domain, codomain, and range of function $f$?

Solution:

  • Domain: $\mathbb{Z}$ (the set of all integers).
  • Codomain: $\mathbb{Z}$ (the set of all integers).
  • Range: $\{0, 1, 4, 9, 16, \dots\}$ (the set of all non-negative perfect squares, $\{x^2 \mid x \in \mathbb{Z}\}$).
Definition 3 (page 141): Addition and Multiplication of Functions (函數加法與乘法)

Let $f_1$ and $f_2$ be functions from $A$ to $\mathbb{R}$. Then $f_1 + f_2$ and $f_1 f_2$ are also functions from $A$ to $\mathbb{R}$ defined by:

$(f_1 + f_2)(x) = f_1(x) + f_2(x)$

$(f_1 f_2)(x) = f_1(x) f_2(x)$

Question:

Let $f_1$ and $f_2$ be functions from $\mathbb{R}$ to $\mathbb{R}$ such that $f_1(x) = x^2$ and $f_2(x) = x - x^2$. What are the functions $f_1 + f_2$ and $f_1 f_2$?

Solution:

• $(f_1 + f_2)(x) = f_1(x) + f_2(x) = x^2 + (x - x^2) = x$.

• $(f_1 f_2)(x) = f_1(x) \cdot f_2(x) = x^2 (x - x^2) = x^3 - x^4$.

Let $f$ be a function from set $A$ to set $B$ and let $S$ be a subset of $A$. The image of $S$ is the subset of $B$ that consists of the images of the elements of $S$.

$f(S) = \{f(s) \mid s \in S\}$

2. One-to-one and Onto Functions (單射、滿射與雙射)

Definition 5 (page 141): One-to-one / Injective Function (單射 / 一對一函數)

A function $f$ is said to be one-to-one, or injective (單射), if and only if $f(x) = f(y)$ implies that $x = y$ for all $x$ and $y$ in the domain of $f$. (Not many-to-one).

A function is called an injection if it is one-to-one.

Example 8:

Determine whether $f: \{a,b,c,d\} \to \{1,2,3,4,5\}$ with $f(a)=4, f(b)=5, f(c)=1, f(d)=3$ is one-to-one.

Answer: Yes, $f$ is one-to-one because no two elements in the domain map to the same element in the codomain.

Example 9:

Determine whether $f(x) = x^2$ from $\mathbb{Z}$ to $\mathbb{Z}$ is one-to-one.

Answer: No, because $f(1) = 1^2 = 1$ and $f(-1) = (-1)^2 = 1$, but $1 \ne -1$. (Note: If domain is $\mathbb{Z}^+$, then $f$ IS one-to-one).

Example 10:

Determine whether $f(x) = x + 1$ from $\mathbb{R}$ to $\mathbb{R}$ is one-to-one.

Answer: Yes, because if $x + 1 = y + 1$, subtracting 1 from both sides gives $x = y$.

Definition 6 & Conditions (page 143): Strictly Increasing / Decreasing (嚴格單調性)

A function $f$ whose domain and codomain are subsets of $\mathbb{R}$ is called:

  • Strictly Increasing (嚴格遞增): if $f(x) < f(y)$ whenever $x < y$.
  • Strictly Decreasing (嚴格遞減): if $f(x) > f(y)$ whenever $x < y$.
Theorem: If a function is either strictly increasing or strictly decreasing, it MUST be one-to-one.
Definition 7 (page 143): Onto / Surjective Function (滿射函數)

A function $f$ from $A$ to $B$ is called onto, or surjective (滿射), if and only if for every element $b \in B$ there is an element $a \in A$ with $f(a) = b$.

A function $f$ is called a surjection if it is onto.

3. Inverse Functions and Composition of Functions (反函數與函數複合)

Definition 9 (page 145): Inverse Function (反函數)

Let $f$ be a one-to-one correspondence (bijection) from the set $A$ to the set $B$. The inverse function of $f$ is the function that assigns to an element $b \in B$ the unique element $a \in A$ such that $f(a) = b$.

The inverse function of $f$ is denoted by $f^{-1}$. Hence,

$f^{-1}(b) = a \iff f(a) = b$

*Important Note: If $f$ is not a one-to-one correspondence, we CANNOT define an inverse function of $f$.

Example 27 (page 150): Prove that if $x$ is a real number, then $\lfloor 2x \rfloor = \lfloor x \rfloor + \lfloor x + 0.5 \rfloor$.

Proof: Write $x = n + r$, where $n \in \mathbb{Z}$ and $0 \le r < 1$. We consider two cases for $r$:

Case 1 ($0 \le r < 0.5$):

• $2x = 2n + 2r$, where $0 \le 2r < 1 \implies \lfloor 2x \rfloor = 2n$.

• $\lfloor x \rfloor = n$.

• $x + 0.5 = n + r + 0.5 < n + 1 \implies \lfloor x + 0.5 \rfloor = n$.

• Thus, $\lfloor x \rfloor + \lfloor x + 0.5 \rfloor = n + n = 2n = \lfloor 2x \rfloor$.

Case 2 ($0.5 \le r < 1$):

• $2x = 2n + 2r = 2n + 1 + (2r - 1)$, where $0 \le 2r - 1 < 1 \implies \lfloor 2x \rfloor = 2n + 1$.

• $\lfloor x \rfloor = n$.

• $x + 0.5 = n + (r + 0.5)$, where $1 \le r + 0.5 < 1.5 \implies \lfloor x + 0.5 \rfloor = n + 1$.

• Thus, $\lfloor x \rfloor + \lfloor x + 0.5 \rfloor = n + (n + 1) = 2n + 1 = \lfloor 2x \rfloor$. $\blacksquare$

Example 28 (page 145/150): Prove or disprove that $\lceil x + y \rceil = \lceil x \rceil + \lceil y \rceil$ for all real numbers $x, y$.

Answer: Statement is FALSE.

Counterexample: Let $x = 0.5$ and $y = 0.5$.

• $\lceil x + y \rceil = \lceil 0.5 + 0.5 \rceil = \lceil 1.0 \rceil = 1$.

• $\lceil x \rceil + \lceil y \rceil = \lceil 0.5 \rceil + \lceil 0.5 \rceil = 1 + 1 = 2$.

Since $1 \ne 2$, the statement does not hold in general.

1. Definitions and Basic Concepts (基本定義與概念)

Definition 1 (page 170): Same Cardinality (相同基數)

The sets A and B have the same cardinality if and only if there is a one-to-one correspondence (一一對應/雙射) from A to B. When A and B have the same cardinality, we write $|A| = |B|$.

Definition 2 (page 170): Cardinality Comparison (基數大小)

If there is a one-to-one function from A to B, the cardinality of A is less than or the same as the cardinality of B and we write $|A| \le |B|$. When $|A| \le |B|$ and they have different cardinality, we write $|A| < |B|$.

Definition 3 (page 171): Countable and Uncountable Sets (可數集與不可數集)

A set that is either finite or has the same cardinality as the set of positive integers ($\mathbb{Z}^+$) is countable (可數的或可列的). A set that is not countable is called uncountable (不可數的).

When an infinite set S is countable, we denote the cardinality of S by $\aleph_0$ (aleph, 希伯來字母的第一個字母).

3. Uncountable Sets & Cantor's Diagonal Argument (不可數集與康托爾對角線法)

Solution: To show that the set of real numbers is uncountable, we suppose that the set of real numbers is countable and arrive at a contradiction. Then, the subset of all real numbers that fall between 0 and 1 would also be countable (because any subset of a countable set is also countable; see Exercise 16).

Under this assumption, the real numbers between 0 and 1 can be listed in some order:

$s_1 = 0.a_{11} a_{12} a_{13} a_{14} \dots$
$s_2 = 0.a_{21} a_{22} a_{23} a_{24} \dots$
$s_3 = 0.a_{31} a_{32} a_{33} a_{34} \dots$
$s_n = 0.a_{n1} a_{n2} a_{n3} a_{n4} \dots$

Form a new real number with decimal expansion $s = 0.a_1 a_2 a_3 a_4 \dots$, where the decimal digits are determined by the following rule:

$a_i = 5$ if $a_{ii} = 4$
$a_i = 4$ if $a_{ii} \neq 4$

Because this new number $s$ differs from every $s_i$ at the $i$-th decimal place ($a_i \neq a_{ii}$), it cannot be any number in the list. Thus, the assumption that all real numbers between 0 and 1 could be listed must be false. Therefore, the set of real numbers is uncountable.

4. Schröder-Bernstein Theorem (施羅德－伯恩斯坦定理)

Schröder-Bernstein Theorem (page 175):

If A and B are sets with $|A| \le |B|$ and $|B| \le |A|$, then $|A| = |B|$. In other words, if there are one-to-one functions $f$ from A to B and $g$ from B to A, then there is a one-to-one correspondence between A and B.

Example 6 (page 175): Show that $|(0,1)| = |(0,1]|$

• (i) $f: (0,1) \to (0,1]$ (one-to-one function): $f(x) = x$

• (ii) $g: (0,1] \to (0,1)$ (one-to-one function): $g(x) = x/2$. Similarly, $|(0,1)| = |(0,1/2)|$.

Named after Ernst Schröder (published flawed proof in 1898), Felix Bernstein (presented proof in 1897), and Richard Dedekind (proof found in notes dated 1887).

Definition 4 (page 175): Computable Function (可計算函數)

A function is computable if there is a computer program in some programming language that finds the values of this function. If a function is not computable, we say it is uncomputable (不可計算的). The set of computer programs is countable.

Question & Formula: Number of Relations (關係總數)

If $|A| = m$, $|B| = n$, how many relations are there from $A$ to $B$?

4. Properties of Relations (關係的四大性質)

(1) Reflexive Relation (自反關係) (page 576)
$(a,a) \in R$ for every element $a \in A$.

所有元素都有到自身的自環 (self-loop)。對角線元素全為 1。

(2a) Symmetric Relation (對稱關係) (page 577)
$(b,a) \in R$ whenever $(a,b) \in R$, for all $a,b \in A$.

若有由 $a$ 到 $b$ 的邊，必有由 $b$ 到 $a$ 的邊。

(2b) Antisymmetric Relation (反對稱關係) (page 577)
$(a,b) \in R$ and $(b,a) \in R \implies a = b$, for all $a,b \in A$.

若 $a \ne b$，則 $(a,b)$ 與 $(b,a)$ 不可能同時存在於 $R$ 中。

(3) Transitive Relation (遞移關係) (page 578)
$(a,b) \in R$ and $(b,c) \in R \implies (a,c) \in R$, for all $a,b,c \in A$.

有路徑長度 2 的起終點必有長度 1 的直接邊。

5. Combining Relations, Composite & Power (關係的組合、複合與次數)

Combining Relations (集合運算組合)

Since relations $R_1, R_2 \subseteq A \times B$ are sets, we can combine them using standard set operations: $R_1 \cup R_2$, $R_1 \cap R_2$, $R_1 - R_2$, $R_2 - R_1$, $R_1 \oplus R_2$.

Example 17 (page 579): $A=\{1,2,3\}$, $B=\{1,2,3,4\}$, $R_1 = \{(1,1), (2,2), (3,3)\}$, $R_2 = \{(1,1), (1,2), (1,3)\}$.

  • $R_1 \cup R_2 = \{(1,1), (1,2), (1,3), (2,2), (3,3)\}$
  • $R_1 \cap R_2 = \{(1,1)\}$
  • $R_1 - R_2 = \{(2,2), (3,3)\}$
  • $R_2 - R_1 = \{(1,2), (1,3)\}$

Example 18 (page 579): $R_1 = \{(a,b) \mid a \text{ has taken course } b\}$, $R_2 = \{(a,b) \mid a \text{ requires course } b \text{ to graduate}\}$.

  • • $R_1 \cup R_2$: student $a$ has taken course $b$ OR requires course $b$ to graduate.
  • • $R_1 \cap R_2$: student $a$ has taken course $b$ AND requires course $b$ to graduate.
  • • $R_1 - R_2$: student $a$ has taken course $b$ BUT course $b$ is NOT required for graduation.
  • • $R_2 - R_1$: student $a$ requires course $b$ to graduate BUT has NOT taken it yet.

Example 19: $R_1 = \{(x,y) \mid x < y\}$, $R_2 = \{(x,y) \mid x > y\}$.

• $R_1 \cup R_2 = \{(x,y) \mid x \ne y\}$, $R_1 \cap R_2 = \emptyset$, $R_1 \oplus R_2 = \{(x,y) \mid x \ne y\}$, $R_1 - R_2 = R_1$, $R_2 - R_1 = R_2$.

Definition 6: Composite of Two Relations (關係的複合) (page 580)

Let $R$ be a relation from set $A$ to set $B$ and $S$ a relation from $B$ to $C$. The composite of $R$ and $S$ is the relation consisting of ordered pairs $(a,c)$, where $a \in A$, $c \in C$, and for which there exists an element $b \in B$ such that $(a,b) \in R$ and $(b,c) \in S$.

Notation: $S \circ R$

$R$ from $\{1,2,3\}$ to $\{1,2,3,4\}$ with $R = \{(1,1), (1,4), (2,3), (3,1), (3,4)\}$.

$S$ from $\{1,2,3,4\}$ to $\{0,1,2\}$ with $S = \{(1,0), (2,0), (3,1), (3,2), (4,1)\}$.

$S \circ R = \{(1,0), (1,1), (2,1), (2,2), (3,0), (3,1)\}$

Definition 7: Power of a Relation (關係的次數) (page 580)

Let $R$ be a relation on the set $A$. The powers $R^n$, $n=1,2,3,\dots$ are defined recursively by:

$R^1 = R \quad \text{and} \quad R^{n+1} = R^n \circ R$

Let $R = \{(1,1), (2,1), (3,2), (4,3)\}$.

• $R^2 = R \circ R = \{(1,1), (2,1), (3,1), (4,2)\}$

• $R^3 = R^2 \circ R = \{(1,1), (2,1), (3,1), (4,1)\}$

• $R^4 = R^3 \circ R = \{(1,1), (2,1), (3,1), (4,1)\}$

• For $n \ge 3$, $R^n = R^3$.

Theorem 1: Characterization of Transitivity by Powers (遞移性定理)

The relation $R$ on a set $A$ is transitive if and only if $R^n \subseteq R$ for $n = 1, 2, 3, \dots$

Part 1: Proof of "If" part ($\impliedby$)

Suppose that $R^n \subseteq R$ for $n=1,2,\dots$. In particular, $R^2 \subseteq R$.

To see that this implies $R$ is transitive, note that if $(a,b) \in R$ and $(b,c) \in R$, then by definition of composite, $(a,c) \in R^2$. Because $R^2 \subseteq R$, this means $(a,c) \in R$. Hence, $R$ is transitive.

Part 2: Proof of "Only If" part ($\implies$) using Mathematical Induction

Assume $R$ is transitive. We prove $R^n \subseteq R$ for all $n \ge 1$ by induction on $n$.

  • Basis Step ($n=1$): $R^1 = R \subseteq R$ is trivially true.
  • Inductive Hypothesis: Suppose $R^n \subseteq R$ holds for a positive integer $n$.
  • Inductive Step: Consider any $(a,b) \in R^{n+1} = R^n \circ R$. By definition of composition, there exists $x \in A$ such that $(a,x) \in R$ and $(x,b) \in R^n$.
    By the inductive hypothesis $R^n \subseteq R$, so $(x,b) \in R$.
    Since $R$ is transitive, $(a,x) \in R$ and $(x,b) \in R$ implies $(a,b) \in R$.
    This proves $R^{n+1} \subseteq R$.

By mathematical induction, $R^n \subseteq R$ for all $n \ge 1$. $\blacksquare$

§ 9.4 Closures of Relations (關係的閉包)

1. Introduction & General Definition (閉包通用定義)

The Closure of Relation $R$ with Respect to Property $P$ (page 597)

Let $R$ be a relation on $A$. $R$ may or may not have some property $P$ (such as reflexive, symmetric, or transitive).

If there is a relation $S$ with property $P$ containing $R$ ($R \subseteq S$) such that $S$ is a subset of every relation with property $P$ containing $R$, then $S$ is called the closure of $R$ with respect to $P$ (關於性質 $P$ 的閉包).

2. Reflexive & Symmetric Closures (自反閉包與對稱閉包)

Reflexive Closure (自反閉包)

Let $\Delta = \{(a,a) \mid a \in A\}$ be the diagonal relation (對角線關係).

Reflexive Closure $= R \cup \Delta$

Example 1: $R = \{(a,b) \mid a < b\}$ on integers $\implies$ Reflexive closure $= R \cup \Delta = \{(a,b) \mid a \le b\}$.

Symmetric Closure (對稱閉包)

Let $R^{-1} = \{(b,a) \mid (a,b) \in R\}$ be the inverse relation.

Symmetric Closure $= R \cup R^{-1}$

Example 2: $R = \{(a,b) \mid a > b\}$ on positive integers $\implies$ Symmetric closure $= R \cup R^{-1} = \{(a,b) \mid a \ne b\}$.

Let $S = R \cup R^{-1}$. We must prove three conditions:

1) Prove $S$ is symmetric:

For any $(a,b) \in S = R \cup R^{-1}$, there are two cases:

i) $(a,b) \in R \implies (b,a) \in R^{-1} \implies (b,a) \in S$.

ii) $(a,b) \in R^{-1} \implies (b,a) \in R \implies (b,a) \in S$. Thus $S$ is symmetric.

2) Prove $R \subseteq S$:

Obvious from $S = R \cup R^{-1}$.

3) Prove $S$ is the smallest such relation ($S \subseteq T$ for any symmetric $T \supseteq R$):

Suppose $T$ is symmetric and $R \subseteq T$. Let $(a,b) \in S = R \cup R^{-1}$.

i) If $(a,b) \in R$, then $(a,b) \in T$ because $R \subseteq T$.

ii) If $(a,b) \in R^{-1}$, then $(b,a) \in R \subseteq T$. Since $T$ is symmetric, $(b,a) \in T \implies (a,b) \in T$.

In both cases $(a,b) \in T$. Thus $S \subseteq T$. $\blacksquare$

A path of length $n \ge 1$ that begins and ends at the same vertex is called a circuit / cycle (迴路).

4. Transitive Closure & Connectivity Relation (遞移閉包與連通關係)

Definition 2: Connectivity Relation $R^*$ (連通關係) (page 600)

Let $R$ be a relation on a set $A$. The connectivity relation $R^*$ consists of the pairs $(a,b)$ such that there is a path of length at least one from $a$ to $b$ in $R$.

$R^* = \bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty} R^n$

Theorem 2: Transitive Closure Theorem (page 601)

The transitive closure of a relation $R$ equals the connectivity relation $R^*$.

1) $R^*$ contains $R$: $R^1 = R \subseteq R^*$ by definition.

2) $R^*$ is transitive: If $(a,b) \in R^*$ and $(b,c) \in R^*$, there exist paths from $a$ to $b$ and $b$ to $c$. Combining them gives a path from $a$ to $c$, so $(a,c) \in R^*$. Thus $R^*$ is transitive.

3) $R^*$ is the smallest transitive relation containing $R$: Suppose $S$ is any transitive relation containing $R$ ($R \subseteq S$). Since $S$ is transitive, $S^n \subseteq S$ for all $n \ge 1$ (by Theorem 1 of Section 9.1). It follows that $S^* \subseteq S$. Since $R \subseteq S$, any path in $R$ is a path in $S$, so $R^* \subseteq S^* \subseteq S$. Consequently, $R^* \subseteq S$. $\blacksquare$

Lemma 1 (Path Length Bound) (page 601)

Let $A$ be a set with $n$ elements, and $R$ be a relation on $A$. If there is a path of length at least one in $R$ from $a$ to $b$, then there is a path with length not exceeding $n$.

Moreover, when $a \ne b$, if there is a path from $a$ to $b$, then there is such a path with length not exceeding $n-1$.

From Lemma 1, the transitive closure $R^*$ of a relation $R$ on a set with $n$ elements is the finite union:

$R^* = R \cup R^2 \cup R^3 \cup \dots \cup R^{n-1}$ if $n > 1$
$R^* = R \phantom{\cup R^2 \cup R^3 \cup \dots \cup R^{n-1}}$ if $n = 1$
$R^* = R \cup R^2 \cup R^3 \cup \dots \cup R^n$ if $n \ge 1$

§ 9.5 Equivalence Relations (等價關係)

1. Definition of Equivalence Relation (等價關係定義)

Definition 1: Equivalence Relation (page 608)

A relation $R$ on a set $A$ is called an equivalence relation (等價關係) if it is:

1. Reflexive (自反)
2. Symmetric (對稱)
3. Transitive (遞移)

Remark: Two elements $a,b$ that are related by an equivalence relation are called equivalent (等價), written $a \sim b$ or $a \, R \, b$.

(a) String Length Relation: $a \, R \, b \iff l(a) = l(b)$ on English strings.
Reflexive: $l(a)=l(a)$; Symmetric: $l(a)=l(b) \implies l(b)=l(a)$; Transitive: $l(a)=l(b) \land l(b)=l(c) \implies l(a)=l(c)$. $\implies$ Equivalence relation.
(b) Example 2 (page 608): $a \, R \, b \iff a = b \text{ or } a = -b \iff |a| = |b|$ on integers $\mathbb{Z}$.
Reflexive, Symmetric, Transitive. $\implies$ Equivalence relation.
(c) Example 3: $a \, R \, b \iff a - b \in \mathbb{Z}$ on real numbers $\mathbb{R}$.
1) $a - a = 0 \in \mathbb{Z} \implies a \, R \, a$.
2) If $a - b = k \in \mathbb{Z}$, then $b - a = -k \in \mathbb{Z} \implies b \, R \, a$.
3) If $a - b = k_1 \in \mathbb{Z}$ and $b - c = k_2 \in \mathbb{Z}$, then $a - c = (a-b) + (b-c) = k_1 + k_2 \in \mathbb{Z} \implies a \, R \, c$. $\implies$ Equivalence relation.
(d) Congruence Modulo $m$ (模 $m$ 同餘) (page 608):
Let $m > 1$ be an integer. Relation $R = \{(a,b) \mid a \equiv b \pmod m\} \iff m \mid (a - b)$.
This divides all integers into $m$ distinct congruence classes. Is an equivalence relation.

2. Equivalence Classes (等價類)

Definition 2: Equivalence Class (page 610)

Let $R$ be an equivalence relation on a set $A$. The set of all elements that are related to an element $a$ of $A$ is called the equivalence class (等價類) of $a$.

$[a]_R = \{s \mid (a,s) \in R\}$

Example 8 (page 610): For $a \, R \, b \iff |a| = |b|$, $[a] = \{-a, a\}$.

• $[7] = \{-7, 7\}$, $[5] = \{-5, 5\}$, $[0] = \{0\}$.

Congruence Modulo $m$ Equivalence Classes:

$[a]_m = \{\dots, a-2m, a-m, a, a+m, a+2m, \dots\}$

For modulo 4:

• $[0]_4 = \{\dots, -8, -4, 0, 4, 8, \dots\}$

• $[1]_4 = \{\dots, -7, -3, 1, 5, 9, \dots\}$

• $[2]_4 = \{\dots, -6, -2, 2, 6, 10, \dots\}$

• $[3]_4 = \{\dots, -5, -1, 3, 7, 11, \dots\}$

3. Equivalence Classes & Partition Theorems (等價類與分割定理)

Theorem 1: Characterization of Equivalence Classes (page 612)

Let $R$ be an equivalence relation on a set $A$. These statements are equivalent:

(i) $a \, R \, b$
(ii) $[a] = [b]$
(iii) $[a] \cap [b] \ne \emptyset$
(i) implies (ii): Suppose $a \, R \, b$. For any $c \in [a]$, we have $a \, R \, c$. Since $a \, R \, b$ and $R$ is symmetric, $b \, R \, a$. Since $R$ is transitive, $b \, R \, a$ and $a \, R \, c \implies b \, R \, c$, so $c \in [b]$. This proves $[a] \subseteq [b]$. Similarly, $[b] \subseteq [a]$. Therefore $[a] = [b]$.
(ii) implies (iii): Assume $[a] = [b]$. Since $R$ is reflexive, $a \in [a]$. Thus $a \in [a] \cap [b]$, so $[a] \cap [b] \ne \emptyset$.
(iii) implies (i): Suppose $[a] \cap [b] \ne \emptyset$. Then there exists $c \in [a] \cap [b]$, meaning $a \, R \, c$ and $b \, R \, c$. By symmetry, $c \, R \, b$. By transitivity, $a \, R \, c$ and $c \, R \, b \implies a \, R \, b$. $\blacksquare$
Partition of a Set (集合的分割) (page 613)

A partition (分割) of a set $S$ is a collection of disjoint nonempty subsets $A_i$ ($i \in I$) of $S$ that have $S$ as their union:

  • 1. $A_i \ne \emptyset$ for all $i \in I$
  • 2. $A_i \cap A_j = \emptyset$ when $i \ne j$ (Disjoint)
  • 3. $\bigcup_{i \in I} A_i = S$

Example 7 (page 613): Let $S = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}$. $A_1 = \{1,2,3\}$, $A_2 = \{4,5\}$, $A_3 = \{6\}$ forms a partition of $S$.

Constructing Relation from Partition: $a \, R \, b \iff a$ and $b$ are in the same subset.

• Ordered pairs: $A_1 \times A_1 \cup A_2 \times A_2 \cup A_3 \times A_3$.

Theorem 2: Fundamental Theorem of Equivalence Relations (page 613)

Let $R$ be an equivalence relation on a set $S$. Then the equivalence classes of $R$ form a partition of $S$.

Conversely, given a partition $\{A_i \mid i \in I\}$ of the set $S$, there is an equivalence relation $R$ that has the sets $A_i$ as its equivalence classes.

1. Basic Terminology & Degrees (基本術語與度數，Page 651 - 654)

Definition 1 (page 651): Adjacent, Incident & Endpoints

Two vertices $u$ and $v$ in an undirected graph $G$ are called adjacent (相鄰的) or neighbors (鄰居) in $G$ if $\{u, v\}$ is an edge of $G$.

If $e = \{u, v\}$, the edge $e$ is called incident (關聯的) with vertices $u$ and $v$, connecting its endpoints (端點) $u$ and $v$.

Definition 3 (page 652): Degree $\deg(v)$ (頂點度數)

The degree of a vertex $v$ in an undirected graph, denoted by $\deg(v)$, is the number of edges incident with it, except that a loop (自環) contributes twice to the degree.

• Isolated (孤立頂點): $\deg(v) = 0$（不與任何頂點相鄰）。
• Pendant (懸掛頂點): $\deg(v) = 1$（恰與一個頂點相鄰）。

Graph $G$ ($\deg(e)=3, \deg(g)=0$)

$e$ 連接 $b, c, f$（$\deg(e)=3$）；$g$ 為孤立頂點（$\deg(g)=0$）；$d$ 為懸掛頂點（$\deg(d)=1$）。

Graph $H$ ($\deg(e)=6, \deg(b)=6$)

$b$ 含 1 個自環（+2）、連 $a$（+1）、連 $c$（+1）、連 $e$（+1）、連 $d$（+1），共 $\deg(b)=6$；$e$ 有 2 條連 $a$、3 條連 $d$、1 條連 $b$，共 $\deg(e)=6$。

The Handshaking Theorem (握手定理，Page 653)

Let $G = (V, E)$ be an undirected graph with $e$ edges. Then:

$2e = \sum_{v \in V} \deg(v)$

Example 3 (page 653): 10 vertices each of degree 6 $\implies 2e = 10 \times 6 = 60 \implies e = 30$.

Theorem 2 (page 653): Even Number of Odd-Degree Vertices

An undirected graph has an even (偶數) number of vertices of odd (奇數) degree.

$2e = \sum_{v \in V_1} \deg(v) + \sum_{v \in V_2} \deg(v)$
($V_1$: 偶度數頂點集；$V_2$: 奇度數頂點集。因左式與 $\sum_{V_1}$ 皆為偶數，故 $\sum_{V_2}$ 必為偶數，推得 $|V_2|$ 為偶數。)

Definitions 4 & 5 & Theorem 3 (page 654): Directed Graph Degrees (有向圖出入度)

For directed edge $(u, v)$: $u$ is adjacent to $v$ ($u$ is the initial vertex 起點), and $v$ is adjacent from $u$ ($v$ is the terminal vertex 終點).

In-degree $\deg^-(v)$ (入度)

以 $v$ 為終點的邊數。

Out-degree $\deg^+(v)$ (出度)

以 $v$ 為起點的邊數。

Loop & Theorem 3

自環對入度與出度各貢獻 $1$；且 $\sum_{v \in V} \deg^-(v) = \sum_{v \in V} \deg^+(v) = |E|$。

2. Special Simple Graphs (特殊簡單圖，Page 655)

Example 5: Complete Graph $K_n$ (完全圖)

Simple graph on $n$ vertices with exactly one edge between each pair of distinct vertices（邊數為 $\binom{n}{2} = \frac{n(n-1)}{2}$）。

Example 6: Cycle $C_n$ (圈圖，$n \ge 3$)

Consists of $n$ vertices $v_1, v_2, \dots, v_n$ and edges $\{v_1, v_2\}, \{v_2, v_3\}, \dots, \{v_{n-1}, v_n\}, \{v_n, v_1\}$。

Example 7: Wheel $W_n$ (輪圖，$n \ge 3$)

Obtained by adding one central vertex to $C_n$ and connecting it to all $n$ vertices of $C_n$（共 $n+1$ 個頂點、$2n$ 條邊）。

Example 8: $n$-Cube $Q_n$ ($n$ 維超立方體)

Has $2^n$ vertices representing bit strings of length $n$; two vertices are adjacent iff their bit strings differ in exactly one bit position。

3. Bipartite Graphs (二分圖，Page 656 - 658)

Definition 5 (page 656): Bipartite Graph (二分圖)

A simple graph $G$ is bipartite if its vertex set $V$ can be partitioned into two disjoint sets $V_1$ and $V_2$ such that every edge connects a vertex in $V_1$ and a vertex in $V_2$（$V_1$ 內部與 $V_2$ 內部皆無邊）。

• Example 9: $C_6$ is bipartite with $V_1 = \{v_1, v_3, v_5\}$, $V_2 = \{v_2, v_4, v_6\}$。

• Example 10: $K_3$ is not bipartite（含奇數長度圈）。

Theorem 4 & Complete Bipartite $K_{m,n}$ (Page 657 - 658)

Theorem 4: A simple graph is bipartite iff it can be 2-colored so no two adjacent vertices share the same color（等價於不存在奇數條相異邊組成的奇環回路）。

Example 13 ($K_{m,n}$): Vertex set partitioned into subsets of size $m$ and $n$, with an edge between every pair of vertices from different subsets（共 $m+n$ 個頂點、$mn$ 條邊）。

Example 9: $C_6$ Bipartite Partition

$V_1 = \{v_1, v_3, v_5\}$，$V_2 = \{v_2, v_4, v_6\}$。

4. New Graphs from Old (由舊圖構造新圖，Page 663 - 664)

Definition 7 (page 663): Subgraph & Proper Subgraph (子圖與真子圖)

A subgraph (子圖) of $G = (V, E)$ is a graph $H = (W, F)$ where $W \subseteq V$ and $F \subseteq E$（$F$ 中的邊端點皆須在 $W$ 中）。若 $H \neq G$，則稱 $H$ 為 $G$ 的 proper subgraph (真子圖)。

Definition 9 & Example 19 (page 664): Union $G_1 \cup G_2$ (圖的聯集)

The union of two simple graphs $G_1 = (V_1, E_1)$ and $G_2 = (V_2, E_2)$, denoted by $G_1 \cup G_2$, is the simple graph with vertex set $V_1 \cup V_2$ and edge set $E_1 \cup E_2$。
